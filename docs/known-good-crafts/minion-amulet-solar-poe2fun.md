# Verified Craft: BIS Minion Solar Amulet (poe2fun, Patch 0.4.0)

A cost-effective BIS amulet craft for minion / summoner builds. The 0.4.0 patch removed Omen of Homogenising Exaltation, forcing a new workflow that uses Dextral Crystallisation + Perfect Essence of Enhancement to add a defensive prefix while cleaning up a suffix slot.

## What this craft makes

**Item class:** Amulet
**Specific base:** **Solar Amulet** (the highest-base-ES amulet, ideal for ES-stacking summoner builds)
**iLvl:** any iLvl that supports T1/T2 Spirit (78+ for T1 Spirit)
**Budget:** mid (requires Fractured base, 2-3 Perfect Essences, multiple omens, some catalysts)
**Final outcome (5 mods):**
- **`+3 to Level of all Minion Skills`** — FRACTURED prefix (the foundation)
- **`+# to Spirit`** (T1/T2) — prefix, chaos-rolled
- **`(20-30)% increased Global Defences`** — prefix, from Perfect Essence of Enhancement (Amulet outcome)
- **`#% increased Energy Shield from equipped Body Armour`** — desecrated Kurgal-family prefix from Omen of the Blackblooded
- **Two T1-T2 resistance suffixes** — from catalyst-boosted Greater Exalted Orbs

## Target affixes

**Prefixes (~4):**
1. **`+3 to Level of all Minion Skills`** — FRACTURED (foundation, never touched)
2. **`+# to Spirit`** (T1/T2 — T1 is ilvl 78)
3. **`(20-30)% increased Global Defences`** (Perfect Essence of Enhancement on Amulet — guide says 20-30%, our authoritative essence data confirms this is the Amulet outcome)
4. **`#% increased Energy Shield from equipped Body Armour`** (Kurgal-family desecrated prefix — chosen from the unveil pool via Omen of the Blackblooded)

**Suffixes (2):**
- **T1/T2 Resistance** (Fire OR Cold OR Lightning) — biased toward landing via Catalyst-boosted Greater Exalt
- **T1/T2 Resistance** (a different element)

## Step-by-step craft

> **Patch 0.4.0 context:** Omen of Homogenising Exaltation was REMOVED in 0.4.0. The Dextral Crystallisation + Perfect Essence of Enhancement combo in step 3 is the new workflow for landing Global Defences as a prefix without losing the fractured foundation.

### Step 1 — The Base (Fractured)
- Buy or self-fracture a **Solar Amulet with `+3 to Level of all Minion Skills` as a FRACTURED modifier**.
- Self-fracture is risky/expensive — typically cheaper to buy directly from trade. Fractured Solar Amulets with this specific mod are a market category.
- State: RARE with at least the fractured `+3 Minion Skills` prefix locked.

### Step 2 — Rolling Spirit
- Apply **Chaos Orbs** repeatedly.
- **Goal:** hit **T1 or T2 `+# to Spirit`** prefix. T1 Spirit is the second-most-important mod after +3 — Spirit caps your auras and minion count, do NOT settle for a low roll.
- **Note:** before proceeding to step 3, ensure the item has at least one OPEN PREFIX and one OPEN SUFFIX slot. If the item is full, use **Orb of Annulment** carefully (the fractured prefix can't be removed but other prefixes/suffixes can).

### Step 3 — The Defence Swap (Dextral Crystallisation trick)
Add the Global Defences prefix while consuming a suffix slot — the deterministic part of the craft.

- Apply **Omen of Dextral Exaltation** (forces next Exalt to add a SUFFIX).
- Use an **Exalted Orb** (this adds a dummy random suffix — we don't care what).
- Apply **Omen of Dextral Crystallisation** (forces next Perfect/Corrupted Essence to remove only a SUFFIX).
- Apply **Perfect Essence of Enhancement** (Amulet outcome: adds `(20-30)% increased Global Defences` as a PREFIX, and per the Crystallisation omen, removes a SUFFIX rather than the random affix it would normally remove).
- **Result:** the Essence forces the removal of the dummy suffix we just exalted (or another random suffix) and adds `(20-30)% increased Global Defences` as a prefix. The fractured `+3 Minion Skills` and the T1/T2 Spirit prefix are both untouched.
- You now have 3 prefixes: `+3 Minion Skills`, `Spirit`, `Global Defences`.

### Step 4 — The Kurgal Desecration
Add the synergy prefix `#% increased Energy Shield from equipped Body Armour` via guaranteed Kurgal-family desecration.

- Apply **Omen of Sinistral Necromancy** (forces Desecration to add only a PREFIX — protects suffixes).
- Apply **Omen of the Blackblooded** (forces Desecration to grant a Kurgal-family modifier).
- Apply **Omen of Abyssal Echoes** (allows you to reroll the revealed options once — safety net).
- Use **Preserved Collarbone** (Desecration item for rare amulets / rings / belts).
- Unveil the modifier and choose the option.
- **Goal:** choose **`#% increased Energy Shield from equipped Body Armour`** from the revealed options. This synergizes massively with a high-ES body armour (paired with Belton's ES Chest craft if you've done that) for summoner survivability.

### Step 5 — Finishing Suffixes (Catalyst Tech)
Fill the two empty suffix slots with high-tier resistances using catalyst-boosted Exalts.

- Apply **Fire / Cold / Lightning Catalysts** to the amulet until **quality is 20%** (use whichever catalyst matches the resistance you most need — or split them).
- Apply **Omen of Catalysing Exaltation** (next Exalted Orb consumes all Catalyst Quality on the item to bias the new modifier toward the catalysed type).
- Use a **Greater Exalted Orb** (adds 1 new modifier with the catalyst-bias active).
- **Why Catalysts?** In PoE2, catalysts increase the "weight" (probability) of rolling their associated tags by 500%. Combined with Omen of Catalysing Exaltation, you are almost guaranteed to hit a high-tier Resistance modifier.
- Repeat the Catalyst → Omen → Exalt sequence for the second suffix slot if needed.

## Summary

By using a Fractured base and manipulating the Suffix/Prefix slots with Omens, you've crafted a Solar Amulet that offers:
- **Offense:** +3 Minion Skills
- **Utility:** High Spirit (caps auras and minion count)
- **Defense:** Global Defences % + Energy Shield from Body Armour + capped resistances

Perfect for any PoE2 minion build in Patch 0.4.0.

## Why this craft works

- **Fractured `+3 Minion Skills`** is the foundation — chaos spam in step 2 can't remove it.
- **Dextral Crystallisation + Perfect Essence of Enhancement** is the new (post-0.4.0) replacement for the removed Omen of Homogenising Exaltation. The dummy-suffix-then-essence-removes-suffix workflow lets you add a prefix without losing prefixes.
- **Omen of the Blackblooded** guarantees Kurgal family on desecration — narrows the unveil pool dramatically so the ES-from-Body-Armour mod is highly likely to appear among the options.
- **Catalyst + Omen of Catalysing Exaltation** is the deterministic way to land T1/T2 resistances. 500% weight boost on a catalysed tag plus the omen forcing the next exalt to use that bias makes resistance suffixes near-guaranteed.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **Couldn't roll T1/T2 Spirit (step 2):** keep chaos spamming. Spirit has decent weight on amulet prefixes but can take many attempts.
- **Item full before step 3:** Orb of Annulment carefully — the fractured `+3` won't be removed, but a desirable mod might. Consider Omen of Sinistral / Dextral Annulment if you want to target the affix slot.
- **Bad Kurgal unveil (step 4):** Omen of Abyssal Echoes gave you one reroll — if both screens are bad, use Omen of Light + Orb of Annulment to safely remove ONLY the desecrated mod, then repeat step 4.
- **Catalyst step didn't land a resistance:** quality is consumed by Omen of Catalysing Exaltation, so reapply catalysts to 20% and retry. Or skip the omen and use Sinistral / Dextral Necromancy + Preserved Collarbone to desecrate a resistance from a chosen family.
