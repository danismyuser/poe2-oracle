# Verified Craft: League-Start Physical Martial Weapon (Belton's Method)

A deterministic, no-risk league-start craft that applies to **every physical martial weapon type** — spears, bows, maces, 2h maces, quarterstaves, talismans (and any future martial weapon GGG adds). Carries a build from end-of-campaign through T16 maps. Belton calls this his "Average Andy" guide — designed for repeatability and ease, not min-maxing.

## What this craft makes

**Item class:** any martial weapon — Spear, Bow, Crossbow, One/Two-Handed Sword/Axe/Mace, Quarterstaff, Flail, Talisman
**Specific bases (best per type):** Akoyan Spear, Obliterator Bow, etc. — pick by base flat phys + base crit + base APS, NOT just highest flat damage
**iLvl:** 82 ideal (unlocks T1 phys% + Surpassing Arrow on bows); 81 acceptable (T1 hybrid phys, +5/+7 skills, T1 flat ele); 77 minimum for T1 IAS; 75 for T1 flat phys + T2 phys%; 72 for T1 crit chance
**Budget:** league-start (cheap currencies, 1 essence, optional 1 fracture)
**Final outcome:** 
- **Worst case** (Akoyan Spear, all desecrated/T2/T3, no exceptional base): **577 pDPS**, 10.8% crit, 1.89 APS, +3 attack skills, 20% extra phys damage gained — equivalent ~692 pDPS with the extra-damage factored in
- **Realistic case** (T1 phys% + 25% quality + T2 IAS + T2 crit): **657 pDPS** equivalent ~789 pDPS
- **Obliterator Bow realistic:** **650 pDPS**, 9.4% crit, 1.36 APS, +3 attack, 20% extra → ~780 pDPS equivalent

Sufficient to carry through all early league and well into mid-tier maps.

## Why physical martial is THE league-start weapon

- **Chameleon** for any attack-based build — most skill gems convert physical to elemental partially or wholly, so one weapon survives build pivots
- **Only damage type that leeches life AND mana inherently** — resource sustain is critical early league
- **Versatile across pure phys / bleed / poison/chaos / elemental** builds with no weapon change
- **Quantitatively strongest scaling** — physical martial has the most local-mod multiplicative scaling of any weapon type (base flat → + crafted flat → × phys% (additive: runes + prefix #2 + prefix #3) → × quality (more multiplier) → ÷2 × APS = pDPS)
- **Quality is uniquely powerful here** — 20% quality on a 1000 pDPS weapon = 1200; 30% = 1300. Quality is a MORE multiplier on phys weapons specifically. **30Q bases are often undervalued and underrepresented in the market** — grab them when affordable
- **Market liquidity** — a consistent buyer segment makes profit-crafting easy

## Modifier priority list

**Prefixes (3):**
1. **% Physical Damage** (additive global multiplier — T1 is 175-200%, ilvl 82)
2. **Flat Physical Damage** (T1 added flat phys damage)
3. **Prefix #3 options** (in priority order):
   - **Hybrid `#% increased Physical Damage, # to Accuracy Rating`** (best for high-end / late-stage crafts especially with 30Q)
   - **`Gain #% of Damage as Extra Physical Damage`** (from Perfect Essence of Abrasion — 20% on 1H, 33% on 2H. Best for transitory league-start crafts because it's deterministic via essence)
   - **Flat elemental damage** (situational — for elemental conversion skills)

**Suffixes (3):**
1. **Critical Hit Chance** (T1: ~+4.4-5%) — crit builds only
2. **+# to Level of all Attack Skills** (+3 from Perfect Essence of Battle on 1H; +5 on 2H)
3. **% Increased Attack Speed** (T1: 27-30%; T1-adjacent: ~18% from desecrated)

**Bow-specific deviations:**
- May prioritize **Surpassing Arrow %** over IAS (more arrows = more hits)
- For builds with +attack-time mods or non-speed-scaling, swap IAS for **Onslaught on Killing Hits with this Weapon** (Perfect Essence of Haste outcome) or a desecrated suffix

## Step-by-step craft (works on every martial weapon)

> **Universal applicability:** the steps below work identically for any physical martial weapon. Where differentiation exists it's in the suffix preference (e.g. Surpassing Arrow on bows, no IAS on Maces) — addressable via desecrated suffixes.

### Step 1 — Acquire the base
- Find or buy an **ilvl 82 normal or magic base** of your chosen weapon type (e.g. Akoyan Spear, Obliterator Bow).
- **Quality note:** if you find a 23-27% quality base affordable, grab it. **DO NOT pay premium for 30Q on a league-start craft** — save 30Q bases for later mirror-tier crafts.

### Step 2 — Make it magic with high ilvl mods
- Apply **Perfect Orb of Transmutation** (white → magic, mod level 70+).

### Step 3 — Augment + Annul loop until T1/T2 % Physical Damage prefix
- Apply **Perfect Orb of Augmentation** (adds 2nd mod, ml 70+).
- If you don't have T1/T2 phys%, **Orb of Annulment** + repeat from step 2.
- **Step 3a (skip-ahead):** if Annulment is prohibitively expensive in early league, buy a magic item that already has T1/T2 phys% on it from trade. Ideally with T3+ crit chance or IAS as the second mod.

> **Slow vs fast weapons:** fast weapons (spears, bows) benefit MORE from flat phys; slow weapons (2h maces) benefit MORE from phys% proportionately. So T1 phys% matters more on a 2h mace than on a spear. Bows roll modifiers as 1H despite being 2H — quivers compensate.

### Step 4 — Greater Essence of Abrasion (magic → rare with guaranteed flat phys)
- Apply **Greater Essence of Abrasion** to the magic item. Result: RARE with the guaranteed T3 flat phys prefix + the existing phys% prefix + random suffixes.
- **Step 4a (optional fracture path):** if Fracturing Orbs are cheap AND you have a desired suffix (T3+ crit or IAS), use a **Preserved Jawbone** to add a desecrated mod (need 4 affixes total) then **Fracturing Orb** for 1/3 chance to hit the desired suffix. Skip this if fractures are expensive — the craft works fine without it.

### Step 5 — Add the 3rd prefix (Gain % Damage as Extra Physical Damage)
- **5A.** If the essence step DIDN'T yield a desirable suffix → skip to **5C**.
- **5B.** If a desirable suffix IS present → apply **Omen of Dextral Exaltation** (forces next Exalt to add a suffix) + **Greater Exalted Orb** (Perfect is overkill for league-start) to add another suffix. Now move to 5C.
- **5C.** Apply **Omen of Dextral Crystallisation** + **Perfect Essence of Abrasion**. This REMOVES a random suffix and ADDS `Gain #% of Damage as Extra Physical Damage` as the 3rd prefix (20% on 1H weapons, 33% on 2H).
- **Why this is great:** the extra-damage is calculated AFTER skill-gem conversion. So on a Lightning Spear build, your physical damage gets converted to lightning by the skill, then the 20% extra is added AS PHYSICAL on top — letting you leech life/mana and break armour even on an elemental build. Strong damage scaling AND massive utility.

### Step 6 — Fill the 3 suffixes

**With a fractured suffix (from step 4a):**
- **Greater Exalted Orb × 1**. If it doesn't add T3+ crit or IAS (whichever isn't fractured), Greater Exalt again.
- If still missing, use whichever is cheaper: **Omen of Dextral Necromancy + Preserved Jawbone** (desecration into suffix) OR **Perfect Essence of Battle**. With 3 suffixes filled, the essence will REMOVE a suffix before adding itself (no Crystallisation omen needed).

**Without a fractured suffix:**
- **Greater Exalt × 1**, check, repeat up to 3 times. If all 3 are bad, use **Perfect Essence of Battle** (+3 attack skills), **Perfect Essence of Haste** (Onslaught chance), and **Omen of Dextral Necromancy + Preserved Jawbone** until you have +3 / crit-or-IAS / Onslaught on Kill.
- **Onslaught fallback:** Onslaught is a GLOBAL conditional buff — 20% global attack speed + significant movement speed when triggered. Worse for raw pDPS (it's not local) so will sell for less, BUT often equal or better in actual gameplay because movement speed matters early league. Sells "at cost or 2× cost" per Belton.

**Desecration trick (no fracture, optimal late-stage):**
- Skip exalting suffixes. Immediately **Abyssal Jawbone** for suffix #1 → use **Omen of Dextral Crystallisation + Perfect Essence of Haste/Battle** to remove the desecrated mod if it's bad. You can do this 3× while guaranteeing Onslaught/+3/desecrated as the suffix end-state.
- With **Omen of Abyssal Echoes** you get 6 screens × 3 = 18 modifier options.
- For bow IAS specifically: pair **Preserved Jawbone + Omen of the Liege (Amanamu) + Omen of Dextral Necromancy** → 2/3 chance per Jawbone+Echoes application to get 18% IAS (desecrated IAS goes up to 18% which is T4-equivalent on most weapons but ~T1-adjacent on bows).

## Why this craft works at league-start

- **100% guaranteed end-state** — no annulment gambling on prefixes (the essence-based prefix #3 is deterministic), no luck-based suffix slamming if you do the desecrated trick
- **No expensive omens required** — uses only Dextral Exaltation, Dextral Crystallisation, Dextral Necromancy, optional Abyssal Echoes. NO Sinistral Erasure ladder, no Sanctification, no Hinekora's Lock
- **Cross-applicable** to every martial weapon — same steps work on spears, bows, maces, 2h maces, quarterstaves, talismans
- **Resale-friendly** — physical weapons have a consistent market segment; can profit-craft for sale or self-use

## Failure modes & recovery

- **Annul too expensive in early league (step 3):** buy a magic base with T1/T2 phys% already on it from trade and skip to step 4. Costs more upfront but eliminates the annul lottery.
- **Bricked desecrated suffix:** use Perfect Essence of Haste with Dextral Crystallisation to overwrite. The Haste essence is your "undo" lever for desecrated suffix slots.
- **Couldn't fracture a suffix:** the no-fracture path still produces a serviceable T16-capable weapon. Don't sweat it.
- **Quality of base less than 20Q:** apply Blacksmith's Whetstones up to 20Q after the craft is complete.
