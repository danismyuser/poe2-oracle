# Verified Craft: League-Start ES Chest (Belton's Method)

A high-EV league-start craft for an Energy Shield body armour — Belton calls this "one of the lowest risk, highest impact, easiest to sell crafts you can do for the first 2-3 weeks almost every league."

## What this craft makes

**Item class:** Body Armour (INT)
**Specific base:** **Vile Robe** is the gold standard (highest base ES of 184); any INT body armour works
**iLvl:** 79+ minimum (to unlock T1 ES — note Belton emphasises 79 is enough; you don't need 82 just for ES)
**Budget:** league-start (first 1-3 weeks of league)
**Final outcome:** 1000-1233 ES chest with 2-3 T1/T2 ES mods + 2 useful suffixes (resistances/intelligence). The exact ES depends on quality (20Q or 30Q) and sockets (2S or 3S) — table below.

## Target affixes

**Prefixes (3 — these are the only 3 of 7 wand prefixes you want):**
1. **`+# to maximum Energy Shield`** (ES Flat) — T1: 96 at ilvl 79, T2: 90 at ilvl 70 — weight 1000
2. **`#% increased Energy Shield`** (ES %) — T1: 110% at ilvl 75, T2: 100% at ilvl 70 — weight 1000
3. **`#% increased Energy Shield, # to maximum Energy Shield`** (ES%/ES Flat Hybrid) — T1: 42%/30 at ilvl 78, T2: 38%/25 at ilvl 60 — weight 1000

There are 7 total prefixes on INT body armour (4 of which are ES). The 4 ES prefixes are the three above PLUS `#% increased Energy Shield and Life Hybrid`. The other 3 non-ES prefixes are Maximum Life, Thorns, and Spirit — none of which you want for a pure-ES chest.

**Suffixes (3 useful):**
1. **Any Elemental Resistance** (Fire/Cold/Lightning, T3+ acceptable) — T1: 41-47% at ilvl 82, T2: 36-42% at ilvl 71 — weight 1000 each
2. **Any Elemental Resistance** (second one)
3. **Resistance / Intelligence / Desecrated mod** (Int: 31-33 at ilvl 74; Faster Start ES Recharge: 51-55% at ilvl 81)

Other usable suffixes: Chaos Resistance (24-27% T1 at ilvl 81, weight 250), Spirit (61 T1 at ilvl 78, weight 100 — niche).

## Step-by-step strategy (recommended path)

> **Don't fracture the body yourself.** Belton's strongest recommendation: buy a clean body armour that already has a fractured flat ES, ES%, or hybrid prefix from trade. They're cheap. OR buy a bricked 4-5 mod chest, annul/desecrate down to 1-3 mods, then fracture yourself.

### Path A — Buy a fractured base (preferred)
1. Trade-search for a Vile Robe (iLvl 79+) with a FRACTURED ES prefix (flat, %, or hybrid — any of the 3 target ES prefixes). These are abundant cheap because the 3 ES prefixes have the highest weights and aren't valuable separately.
2. State: RARE with 1-3 affixes, 1 of which is the fractured ES mod.

### Path B — Self-fracture
1. Buy a cheap 4-5 mod rare Vile Robe with the wrong mods.
2. Apply **Orb of Annulment** to remove unwanted mods until you have 3-4 mods total — at least one of which is one of the 3 target ES prefixes.
3. Optionally desecrate-remove with Omens to remove specific mod types.
4. Once you have a clean rare with the target ES prefix, apply **Fracturing Orb** (requires 4+ mods on the rare — add suffixes first if needed). The fracture lands on a random mod; you're aiming for ~1/3 chance it hits the ES prefix you want.
5. If the fracture missed (locked a useless mod instead), sell the result and try another.

### After fracturing — main craft
6. The fractured prefix is now locked. Apply **Greater Chaos Orb** OR continue chaos-spamming to land the other 2 ES prefixes. Because ES mods are all 1000 weight and there are only 4 ES prefixes (and 3 non-ES), the natural roll rate is favourable.
7. Use **Omen of Dextral Erasure** + **Greater Chaos** to protect suffixes if you've already landed useful resistances; reroll prefixes.
8. Once all 3 ES prefixes are landed (or 2 ES + something acceptable), shift to suffixes.

### Suffix work — easy path
9. **Greater Exalted Orb × 3** to fill suffix slots. All resistances are 1000 weight at all tiers, so you're likely to land 2-3 useful resistances naturally.
10. If a slot is bad, **Orb of Annulment** to remove and retry.
11. **Alternative**: Use **Omen of Dextral Necromancy** + **Preserved Rib** to desecrate a random suffix into a Desecrated mod. If you brick it, retry up to 3x.

### Desecrated mod swap trick (advanced)
- If you want a SPECIFIC desecrated mod (e.g. Spirit Reservation Efficiency from Amanamu):
  - Apply **Perfect Essence of Seeking** with **Omen of Dextral Crystallisation** to your last bad suffix. This replaces it with `Hits have reduced critical strike chance against you` (the Perfect Essence of Seeking outcome for body armour).
  - Then apply **Essence of the Abyss** with **Omen of Dextral Crystallisation** + **Preserved Rib** to swap the crit-bonus reduction for a Desecrated mod with the family/omen of your choice.
  - You can do this repeatedly — the crit-bonus essence is the "undo" mechanism.
- If you bricked all 3 suffixes during desecration attempts: SELL THE BASE (someone will buy a 900-1000+ ES chest for league-start) and restart. **Don't get Omens of Light / Whittling involved — bad EV.**

## Why this is the easiest craft in the game

- **All 4 ES prefixes have weight 1000** — highest of any prefix family.
- **All ES tiers have weight 1000** — no tier-bias on top of the mod-bias.
- **Highest ilvl for 3×T1 ES is only 79** (not 82) — bases are abundant.
- **All elemental resistances are 1000 weight at all tiers** — suffix work is trivial.
- **T1 elemental resistance is always ilvl 82** — once you "graduate to whittling crafting" you literally cannot avoid T1 res.
- **Prefixes have no desecrated-exclusive mods** — all reveals are organic, meaning desecration on prefix slot doesn't help. Save desecration for the suffix flex slot.
- **Vile Robe + 0 other ES gear = 5000+ ES** — strong enough on its own to justify the craft for self-use OR resale.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **Bricked suffix during desecration:** Use Perfect Essence of Seeking (crit-bonus reduction on body) + Dextral Crystallisation to overwrite the bricked desecrated mod. Then retry desecration. The crit-bonus essence is your "undo".
- **All 3 suffixes bricked, can't recover:** Sell the base — even bricked the chest has 900-1000 ES which sells fast. EV beats trying to fix with Omens of Light / Whittling.
- **Fracturing Orb landed on a useless mod:** Sell the result, try another. Cheap loss.
- **Couldn't afford 30Q / exceptional base:** T2/T2/T2 20Q 2S still gives 983 ES — strong enough. Don't chase perfection on league-start.
